How happy is your world?

Rate countries, guess who ranks higher, tell us why — then see how your instincts stack up against the World Happiness Report data and against other players.

What you'll do

  1. Grade countries on how happy you think their people are.
  2. Take a -question “which is happier?” quiz.
  3. Explain, in your own words, why some countries rank higher.
  4. See your results in charts and compare with previous players.

Part 1 · Grade the happiness

Move each slider to show how happy you think the average person in that country is, from 0 (least happy imaginable) to 10 (happiest imaginable). Sliders turn solid once you touch them.

Part 2 · Which country is happier?

For each pair, pick the country you think has the higher happiness score.

Part 3 · Why do some countries rank higher?

In a few sentences, what do you think makes people in some countries happier than in others?
